Our Story

From One Truck to a Global Network

Founded in 1999 with a single truck and an unwavering commitment to reliability, LOGISTICA has grown into one of the world's most trusted logistics providers. What began as a small freight operation in New York is now a global network spanning 60+ countries, serving over 2,000 companies.

Our mission has never changed: deliver on time, every time. We combine decades of industry expertise with cutting-edge technology to provide supply chain solutions that keep businesses moving forward.

Our Mission

To be the most reliable logistics partner for businesses that demand performance and transparency across their entire supply chain.

Our Vision

A world where supply chains are seamless, sustainable, and intelligent — connecting every corner of the globe with precision.

Core Values

What Drives Us Forward

Our values shape every decision, every shipment, and every client relationship.

Reliability

We deliver on our promises. When we commit to a transit time, we meet it — period. Our 99% on-time rate speaks for itself.

Innovation

Partnership

Your success is our success. We build long-term relationships, not transactional contracts, and adapt to your evolving needs.

Sustainability

Safety

Speed

Time-sensitive deliveries are our specialty. We optimize every link in the chain to minimize transit times without compromising quality.
