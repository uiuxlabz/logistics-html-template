Our Story

From One Truck to a Global Network

Founded in 1999 with a single truck and a relentless commitment to on-time delivery, LOGISTICA has grown into a global logistics powerhouse operating across 60+ countries. What began as a local freight service in New York is now an integrated supply chain partner trusted by over 2,000 companies worldwide.

Our journey has been defined by innovation, reliability, and an unwavering focus on our clients' success. From adopting GPS tracking in 2005 to launching our AI-powered route optimization platform in 2022, we have always stayed ahead of the curve.
